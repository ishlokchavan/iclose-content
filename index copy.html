Honest answers

The questions you'd
actually want to ask.

No marketing fluff. Just the things every reasonable person wonders before joining something pre-launch.

Why should I trust a pre-launch platform?

Fair question. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and commits to nothing. Founding-member pricing is locked in only when we open — and you can walk away if it doesn't deliver.

How is "up to 100%" actually possible?

iClose isn't a traditional agency taking a cut. Higher plans charge a flat membership instead of a commission split. The platform earns through subscriptions, not your closed deals.

I'm not a broker. Can I still earn?

Yes. Lawyers, wealth managers, executives, and well-networked individuals can refer clients and earn a real referral commission — no license needed.

What if my area isn't covered yet?

We launch with Dubai's major communities and add coverage continuously. If your area isn't there at launch, tell us — we prioritize coverage based on demand.

Do I need to leave my current agency?

Not for the Explorer tier — it works alongside your existing role. Operator and Entrepreneur tiers include visa and full back-office, designed for closers going independent.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep your learning history, referral records, and earnings. We don't lock anyone in. Annual billing means you've already paid for the year — use it through the period.
